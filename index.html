About

I'm a BS Data Science student at IMSciences, Peshawar, currently working as a Data Analyst at the Right to Public Services Commission, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, where I build reporting pipelines and dashboards that support public-sector decision-making. Alongside that, I do freelance data analytics and visualization work on Fiverr and Upwork — 19 and 17 months respectively — across e-commerce, sports, and space-mission datasets.

My research side focuses on deep learning and computer vision for high-dimensional, real-world data: self-supervised representation learning, model calibration and uncertainty estimation, domain generalization, and ensemble methods. I'm first author on six manuscripts spanning medical imaging (CBCT, histopathology, chest X-ray, retinal imaging, ECG) and applied ML (diabetes prediction via stacking ensembles). I'm looking to extend this into graduate research as an RA or through a funded fellowship.
